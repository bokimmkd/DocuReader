# W Reader Help

## Open documents and attachments

Open document uses the Android file picker. W Reader reads PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV, PPTX, EPUB, RTF, ODT, JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP locally. No W Reader account is required. AI, PSD, CDR and legacy DOC/XLS/PPT are not supported.

From My Files, Downloads or an email/message attachment, choose Open with → W Reader when Android offers the app selector. Just once opens this file; Always makes it the default for that file type where Android permits. Another app's internal preview, an existing default or an incorrect MIME type can affect the selector. Share → W Reader is also supported.

## Home, Library and Recent

Home format buttons open matching Library items; they do not scan the whole phone. Library → Add keeps an offline copy, reveals it and preserves the original. Recent contains opened documents. Clear Recent History retains favorites, saved Library copies and PDFs saved from W Reader. App-private copies can be lost when clearing app storage or uninstalling.

## Names, categories and tags

Use a Library document's menu to rename, choose a category from the dropdown or add a New category, and add comma-separated tags. Choose Uncategorized to remove the category from a document. If Library looks empty, check All, All categories, All tags and the search text. Search finds names, categories and tags. Sharing uses the name shown in W Reader. Renaming changes the app's title and shared attachment name, not the original source file.

## Read and find text

Use the reader zoom and Find controls. PDF page position can be remembered. Image-only scans need OCR for text search; OCR is not included. TXT supports UTF-8, BOM UTF-16 and legacy Windows-1251 text up to 8 MB. CSV supports commas, semicolons or tabs and quoted fields; limits are 10,000 rows, 100 columns and 100,000 cells. CSV is a reading view and does not calculate formulas.

## Office, books and image limits

DOCX and XLSX layout may differ from Microsoft Office. Excel uses cached formula values, prints the selected worksheet and refuses incomplete or excessively wide output. PowerPoint is a slide reading view of text, tables and embedded images: exact theme/positioning, animations, video, charts and SmartArt are not reproduced. ODT and EPUB use a reflowed reading view with text, tables and embedded images; original fonts, advanced layout, scripts and interactive content are not reproduced. DRM/encrypted EPUB and ODT are unsupported. RTF is a text view with Unicode support; embedded objects and advanced formatting are omitted. Image preview is optimized to 2048 px with orientation preserved; the original remains unchanged. Large or malformed documents show an error instead of silently truncating. Archive inputs are limited to 24 MB compressed / 48 MB expanded, with 300 chapters/slides maximum.

## Print any supported file

Tap the printer at the top right of the reader. Android's system print preview opens. Choose a printer, page size, orientation and pages, then print. Your printer may require its manufacturer's print service. Printing has no full-screen advertisement or paid feature gate. Print layout is independent of reader zoom. For Excel, select the intended worksheet first.

## Convert to PDF

In Library, open a supported non-PDF document's menu → Convert to PDF. You can also use Tools → Convert to PDF or the PDF icon in a non-PDF reader. Choose a name and portrait/landscape, then Save PDF. W Reader creates the PDF locally and opens the Android file-save picker. Choose your folder and Save. A PDF saved dialog then offers Open, Share and Print. The saved PDF is added to Library with its chosen filename; no printer is needed to save. The original document stays unchanged. Canceling the folder picker does not report save success. For Excel, select the intended worksheet in the reader before saving. Keep offline copy creates an additional private copy if you want protection against the destination provider becoming unavailable.

PDF conversion uses bundled portable fonts for Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and common mathematical symbols. Layout may differ from the original Office file. If a character is unsupported or a table cannot fit safely, W Reader reports this instead of silently omitting content; use the separate Print button and choose Save as PDF for that document. Images are fitted onto one PDF page with margins. Print opens Android printing only when explicitly selected.

## PDF page tools

In Library, open a PDF's document menu → PDF tools to Add, Delete, Extract or Organize pages, or Stamp PDF. Add opens a picker for the PDF to import; Extract asks which pages to keep; Delete opens the page grid where you select pages and tap Delete. You can also open the PDF and use Tools. Save creates a new PDF copy; open or share that saved output. Check every page before sending important documents.

## Ad-free Lifetime and Restore

The free app has a small bottom banner. Ad-free Lifetime is a single Google Play purchase that removes W Reader banners permanently for that valid purchase. It is not a subscription. The displayed price comes from Google Play in your account's currency; checkout shows applicable taxes. Restore Purchase checks the same Google Play account. Pending/cancelled payments do not unlock ad-free. No W Reader login is needed. Contact support if a confirmed purchase is not restored.

## App updates

W Reader checks Google Play when opened or resumed and retries failures without blocking documents. When Play reports a newer version, Update available offers Update or Later. Update requests a Google Play flexible download while you keep reading. Download progress is shown; Restart to update appears after Play confirms the download is ready. Save your work before restarting. Later hides the offer for the current visit. Settings → Check for updates retries manually. Updates require the correct owning Google Play account, tester eligibility and a higher available version. An old installed version keeps its old update mechanism until upgraded.

## Rating and privacy

Rate app opens W Reader in Google Play. An optional native review request may be attempted after seven days and use on three different days, with a thirty-day cooldown; Google controls whether it appears. Privacy Policy is readable inside W Reader without internet. Ad privacy options opens Google's available consent controls.

## Library selection, categories and unavailable files

Library keeps its type, category, tags, search, sort and scroll position when you return from a reader. Long-press a document, or use Select in its menu, to select multiple files. All shown selects only the current filtered results. Assign a category, add/remove tags, or Remove from Library. Removal affects the list only; original files and private offline copies are not deleted. Use Undo in the confirmation message to restore removed entries. Manage categories with the folder button next to Add. Enter New category and tap + to create it; duplicate names are rejected. Deleting a category moves its entries to Uncategorized.

Red rows with “File unavailable” mean the file cannot currently be reached, not necessarily that it was deleted. Retry, Locate file to restore access while keeping metadata, or Remove from Library. Checks apply to visible rows; W Reader never scans your whole phone. Cloud providers may need internet or renewed permission. In an Images list, swipe at fit zoom or use arrows to browse the current filtered order. When zoomed, pan/pinch takes priority. Share, PDF export and Print always apply to the current image.

## Book mode

Files open in their normal document view. In Library, open a PDF, EPUB or TXT document's menu → Book mode. EPUB/TXT readers also have a book icon. For EPUB/TXT, Aa offers font size, line spacing, margins and System/Light/Sepia/Dark backgrounds. Pinch with two fingers to increase/decrease text size, synchronized with Aa; text reflows to the screen width. Use Next/Previous or horizontal swipes in Page turns mode; Scroll mode also allows ordinary vertical reading. Page numbers describe screenfuls and change with layout. Progress and up to 30 bookmarks per book stay on this phone. Remember reading position controls automatic position restoration. Book actions opens bookmarks, shares the original or returns to Document view for printing/PDF export. PDF Book mode retains the original page layout and adds page bookmarks, a bookmark list and Previous/Next controls. In the PDF Share menu, choose Document view to return to the normal tools. PDF zoom uses two fingers; there is no Zoom slider. Reading bookmarks are local app data and do not modify the source PDF. No bookstore, account, cloud sync or Amazon/DRM access is included.

## Sign PDFs

Choose Tools → Sign PDF and select a PDF, or choose Sign PDF in a PDF's Library menu. In an open PDF, use Tools or Share options → Sign PDF to start on the current page. Draw your signature with a finger and use Clear / Retry when needed. Save for reuse is optional: it keeps a private copy only on this device. My signature inserts that saved copy; Draw new signature lets you draw again. Delete saved signature removes the reusable copy, not signatures in PDFs already exported.

Choose Place signature, select the page, drag the blue signature box and adjust Size. Pinch the page to zoom; drag outside the signature to pan a zoomed page. Fit restores the page preview. Save signed copy creates a new PDF in the location you choose, with Open, Share and Print available afterward. The original remains unchanged. This places a visible handwritten signature; it is not certificate-based digital signing. Convert Word or Excel to PDF first. Encrypted PDFs must be opened as an unprotected copy before editing.

## Stamp and password copies

In an open PDF choose Tools → Stamp PDF, or use Library → document menu → PDF tools → Stamp PDF. Choose the stamp type, color and position from dropdown menus; you can enter custom text. Choose the current page or all pages and an optional date. The Library shortcut previews page 1; the reader shortcut uses the current visible page. Preview the actual stamped page, then Save copy to your chosen folder. Open, Share and Print are offered after saving. Text and page content remain in the PDF; advanced forms/signatures may be affected by PDF editing. Stamping an encrypted source is not supported.

In an open PDF, tap the Share icon → Share file for ordinary sharing, or Share with password to create a separate AES-256 PDF copy after entering and confirming a 6–64-character password. Share with password is also available from a PDF's Library menu → PDF tools. Original files stay unchanged. Passwords are not saved in Library metadata. Send the password to the recipient separately. Already protected or unsupported PDFs show an error; ordinary Share remains available. Protecting a copy does not change earlier copies you already shared.

## Advertising

Free Library shows an inline banner after document 5, then 15, 25 and so on, without a second bottom banner there. Inline ads hide during selection. Other screens retain the small bottom banner. Ads never delay opening a document. Ad-free Lifetime removes all W Reader ad placements.

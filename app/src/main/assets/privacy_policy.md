# W Reader — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

W Reader is an Android document reader for PDF, DOCX, XLSX, TXT, CSV, PPTX, EPUB, RTF, ODT and JPG/PNG/WebP images. This policy describes how the application handles information.

## Files and local data

- Documents you select are opened and processed on your device. W Reader does not upload document contents, extracted text, or filenames to W Reader servers.
- When you choose **Keep offline copy**, a copy is saved in app-private storage on your device. The source file is not modified.
- Recent, Favorites, categories, tags, and last-reading positions are stored locally. Clear Recent History removes history metadata only, not original documents.
- You can remove app-private data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may delete app-private copies; it does not delete your original files outside the app.

## Sharing and printing

Sharing or printing is user-initiated. If you share a document, Android transfers it to the destination app you choose. If you print, Android or your selected print service may process the document. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Advertising and in-app purchases

Version **0.1.5** introduces a small Google AdMob banner. There are no full-screen ads before opening a document. The earlier **0.1.4** closed-test build has advertising disabled.

Google Mobile Ads and its partners may collect and share approximate location derived from an IP address, device or other identifiers (including advertising ID when available), interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostics such as crash information. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. These services do not receive your document contents, extracted text, or filenames from W Reader.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent before advertising is requested. You can revisit available choices using **Settings → Ad privacy options**. Ad availability and personalization depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's serving decisions. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising controls](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

Ad-free Lifetime is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app stores purchase entitlement locally, queries Google Play to restore purchases, and acknowledges confirmed purchases. No W Reader account or registration is required. Documents and filenames are not sent to Google Play billing.

## Internet access

Document-reading tools work offline. Advertising, consent messages, and external pages require an Internet connection. If consent or advertising is unavailable, you can continue reading documents. Document contents are not uploaded by W Reader.

## Diagnostics and analytics

W Reader has no separate publisher-hosted analytics or diagnostic upload service. Google Mobile Ads may process advertising-related usage and diagnostic information as described above. Android, Google Play, and the operating system may independently collect crash or installation information under their own policies. W Reader does not deliberately include document text or filenames in diagnostic messages.

## Security and third-party components

W Reader uses local document-rendering components and Android system file-provider mechanisms. It does not require a W Reader account. We strive to process only the files that you explicitly open or choose to keep.

## Children

W Reader is a general-purpose productivity tool and not specifically designed for children.

## Changes

If we change data handling, we will update this policy before the corresponding application release.

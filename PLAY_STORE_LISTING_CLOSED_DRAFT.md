## Short description (under 80 characters)

## Full description

W Reader is a simple, practical document reader for Android. Open PDFs, Word (DOCX) and Excel (XLSX) files from your device and supported file-sharing apps, without registering for an account.

**Read documents quickly**
- Open PDF, DOCX and XLSX documents locally.
- Use zoom and search in supported files.
- See recent documents and mark favorites for quick access.
- Organize your files in a local Library with categories and tags.

**Useful PDF tools**
- Select, rearrange, rotate or delete pages in a PDF.
- Add pages from another PDF and extract selected pages.
- Save your changes as a **new PDF**, leaving the original unchanged.
- Share your documents and print supported previews.

**Simple and private by design**
- Your documents are processed on your device.
- No W Reader account or cloud upload is needed.
- Choose which documents to keep as offline copies.

This early version is available for Closed testing. Word and Excel display may differ from their desktop application layout, and some advanced spreadsheet features are not supported. DOC and XLS legacy formats are not included.
